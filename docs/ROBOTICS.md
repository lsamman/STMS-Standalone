# Robotics

All are Breaking Ground pistons. The slider is hidden while a piston is Locked; travel is negative. Turn autostrut off on them and on their loads.

The parts that came from the HLS keep their names. They were made for its airlock and cargo section, which are not in this fork, so they also attach to any surface.

| Part | Use |
|---|---|
| **Elevator** (`STMS_HLS_Lift`) | Lower Deck, then Target Extension to -22.3. It rode rails on the HLS hull; here it has only its own top piece |
| **Elevator (Cable)** (`STMS_HLS_LiftCable`) | Run Cage Out, then Target Extension to -22.3. The cables are stretched by `Plugins/STMS.dll` |
| **Crane Frame** (`STMS_HLS_Crane`) | Stands by a door, Cargo Winch Hoist under its crossbar; -4.4 leans it out |
| **Cargo Floors** (`STMS_HLS_FloorMid`, `_FloorTop`) | Carry loads above each other and lower them: -4.45 and -5.74 |
| **Cargo Tray** (`STMS_HLS_Tray`) | On a floor's top node; -4.1 slides the load out |
| **Cargo Winch** (`SEP_CW_Boom`, `_Hoist`, `_Platform`) | Boom under a ceiling, hoist under the boom, platform under the hoist: boom -4.4, hoist to -29.1 |
| **ITS crane** | See [ITS.md](ITS.md) |

The **Block 3 Landing Leg** (`STMS_Ship_Leg`) attaches to any 5.6 m barrel. The **Crew Access Arm** and **Ship QD Arm** fit StarshipLaunchExpansion's tower.
